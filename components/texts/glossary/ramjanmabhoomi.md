title: "Ramjanmabhoomi"

---
Ramjanmabhoomi literally translates to the birthplace of Lord Rama. It refers to the right-wing Hindu nationalist violence that was mobilized by leading political figures, that eventually culminated in the destruction of the Babri mosque, alleged to be the original site of the Hindu God's birth. The fall of the mosque was also followed by a surge in violence against Muslims and in many ways the existing political landscape in India can be traced to the Ramjanmabhoomi movement. Of late, temples and shrines have mushroomed in and around there several medieval Indo-Islamic structures. The Bhadrakali and Bahucharma temples around the Bhadra fort complex exist in relative harmony with another but the growth in patronage in the recent years cannot be divorced from this larger historical context.
